SCROLL OF GUIDANCE

MOVE — touch and drag anywhere to bend your path. You strike the nearest foe on your own.

DASH — a burst of footwork; you cannot be harmed mid-step.
SKILL — a quick secondary form. Costs a little chi.
SPECIAL — your art's signature form. It drinks deeply of your chi.
ULTIMATE 極 — felling foes charges the gold ring. When it is full, unleash devastation.

FUSE — champions drop bending scrolls. Claim a second art and the two combine into something new.
REDIRECT — dash through an enemy's missile to catch it and hurl it back, twice as hard.
OMENS & PACTS — eclipses, full moons and a crimson comet reshape the field. Stone shrines offer pacts: a gift, for a price.
THE LAND — earthbenders rip rocks from the ground, fire leaves burning embers, water flows strongest near the ponds.

SURVIVE — hunger gnaws at the patient. Forage berry bushes, take meat from the fallen. Night belongs to the spirits; every third dawn brings a champion of the enemy.

GROW — experience flows from the defeated. Each level, choose one of three teachings. Spirit shards ✦ persist beyond death — spend them at the Shrine.
